2. HYPOTHESIS TESTING
--------------------
Three main hypothesis tests were performed:

a) Gender Difference in MAXFWT:
   Two Sample t-test Results:
   - t-statistic: -1.1929
   - p-value: 0.2364
   - Degrees of freedom: 81
   - 95% Confidence Interval: [-9.181, 2.299]
   - Mean MAXFWT (Male): 50.68
   - Mean MAXFWT (Female): 54.12
   Conclusion: No significant difference in MAXFWT between males and females (p > 0.05)

b) Lead Level Impact (Ld72):
   Welch's T-test Results (High vs Low Ld72):
   - t-statistic: -3.0115
   - p-value: 0.9971
   - Degrees of freedom: 25.61
   - 95% Confidence Interval: [-16.101, Inf]
   - Mean MAXFWT (High Ld72): 44.00
   - Mean MAXFWT (Low Ld72): 54.28
   Conclusion: No significant difference in MAXFWT between high and low Ld72 groups

c) Interaction Analysis (Gender and Lead Type):
   ANOVA Results:
   - F-statistic: 2.72
   - p-value: 0.0145
   - Degrees of freedom: 7 (Group), 74 (Residuals)
   - Significant at α = 0.05 level

   Post-hoc Tukey HSD Results (Significant Differences):
   - Male Type4 vs Male Type1: p = 0.005 (significant)
   - Other pairwise comparisons were not statistically significant

Note: 
1. The gender difference test showed no significant difference in MAXFWT scores
2. The lead level impact test did not show significant differences between high and low Ld72 groups
3. The ANOVA revealed significant differences across gender and lead type combinations
4. Post-hoc analysis identified specific significant differences between certain groups
